Overview

About This Service

In simple wordsData analysis means using numbers to answer a question. We do it with Python and explain the results.

You give us your data and your question. We clean the data, look for patterns, run the right tests, and tell you what the results mean in plain words.

You also get the Python code with comments, so you can follow every step.

Deliverables

What You Get

Data cleaning

Missing values, duplicates, and formats handled and documented.

Exploratory analysis

Summary statistics and charts that show patterns in the data.

Statistical tests

Tests such as t-tests, regression, or ANOVA, chosen to fit your question.

Findings write-up

Results explained in plain language.

Is it for you?

Who It's For

  • Students with a data analysis assignment

  • Researchers who need help analyzing their data

  • Anyone learning Python for data work

Simple process

How It Works

  1. 1

    Step 1: Share the data

    Send your dataset and your research question or brief.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Clean and explore

    We clean the data and explore it with summary statistics and charts.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Analyze

    We run the statistical tests that fit your question.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Explain the results

    You receive a commented notebook and a plain-language write-up.

Before we start

What to Send Us

  • Your dataset, or where to find it
  • The assignment brief or research question
  • Required tools or libraries
  • Your deadline
